# HAVK Data Visualization Sprint

This repository contains the official materials for the HAVK Data Visualization Sprint, organized by HAVK at UNC Charlotte.

The sprint challenged participants to analyze and visualize real datasets from fields such as AI, Cybersecurity, Biology, and Engineering.  
Teams worked under time constraints to identify meaningful patterns, communicate insights, and present their findings to a panel of judges.

---

## Event Overview

- **Event Date:** November 3, 2025  
- **Duration:** 5:30 PM – 9:00 PM  
- **Hosted by:** HAVK, University of North Carolina at Charlotte  
- **Format:** Team-based (1–2 participants)  
- **Tools:** Open choice — Python, R, Excel, Tableau, or any visualization platform  

**Schedule**
- 5:15 PM — Dataset release  
- 5:30–6:00 PM — Rules and introduction  
- 6:00–8:00 PM — Analysis and visualization period  
- 8:00–9:00 PM — Team presentations and judging  

---

## Objective

The goal of this sprint was to simulate a realistic, time-bound data science challenge where participants:
1. Clean and interpret raw datasets  
2. Build visual representations of key findings  
3. Present insights clearly and concisely under limited time  
4. Apply critical thinking across disciplines  

The event was designed to help students strengthen technical and analytical skills relevant to careers in AI, Cybersecurity, Data Science, and Research.

---

## Judging Criteria
Submissions were evaluated on:
- **Insight:** Depth and originality of conclusions  
- **Clarity:** How effectively findings were communicated  
- **Creativity:** Uniqueness of the approach  
- **Technical Execution:** Soundness of methods used  
- **Interpretation:** Ability to connect results to real-world meaning  

---

## Notebook Reference

The included **starter notebook** provides a structured reference for participants.  
It outlines recommended steps such as:
- Loading and inspecting CSV files  
- Detecting missing or inconsistent data  
- Exploring patterns between variables  
- Building and refining data visualizations  
- Preparing a concise presentation  

The notebook is not meant to be followed step-by-step, but to help participants design their own workflow.

---

## Outcomes

The HAVK Data Visualization Sprint brought together participants from multiple disciplines to practice analytical reasoning, visualization, and storytelling with data.  
The competition emphasized clarity of communication, practical skills, and collaboration under pressure.
